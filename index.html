Volume 01:
The Silence of Fabric.

Derived from the Meitei words for 'fabric fragments', Phigai honors the traditional practice of upcycling textile waste into architectural silhouettes.

Imphal, Manipur Est. 2018

Born from fragments,
defined by purpose.

Phigai translates to 'fabric scraps' in Manipuri. Our journey began with the belief that nothing is ever truly finished—only repurposed. We blend ancestral handloom techniques with a brutalist, modern silhouette.

We don’t believe luxury should be a shout; it is the silent weight of heavy-weight cotton, the precision of a drape, and the stillness of a well-crafted uniform.
