# Phigai Brand Narrative

## The Name
"Phigai" (Pronounced: Phi-Gai)
Derived from the Meitei (Manipuri) words for 'Fabric' and 'Scraps'. It honors the traditional practice of upcycling textile waste into high-quality, long-lasting garments.

## Core Values
1. **Ancestral Futurism**: Merging traditional handloom techniques (like the Pumyat weave) with brutalist, oversized modern silhouettes.
2. **Silent Luxury**: Designing for the "Quiet Gen." No logos, no noise—just high-GSM fabrics and precise tailoring.
3. **Slow Fashion**: Small batches, ethical manufacturing in Manipur, and pieces designed to age with the wearer.

## Visual Direction
- **Typography**: High-contrast serif (Cormorant) for an editorial vibe; clean sans (Inter) for body.
- **Palette**: Cream `#F5F5F0`, Charcoal `#2C2C2B`, Muted Beige `#D9D2C5`.
- **Photography Style**: Film grain, high shadows, brutalist architecture backdrops, focused on texture and drape.
